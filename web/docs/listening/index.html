Two Bass presets on their headings' edges, and the voices' budget refit

Two of the owner's decisions in web/docs/after-stage-5.md that need an ear. Every take is the instrument itself, the core rendered offline through core/tests/tools/render_cpp.cpp, so a take is the same to the bit every time it is made. Nothing here changes the instrument: the candidates are setups laid over the presets, and the refit is a scratch copy of the core's headers.

Each take is 6.2 seconds, 16-bit at 44.1 kHz, and the left channel alone. The instrument's two channels are the figure's two lanes and are not a stereo image of one sound (on the Bass under the tine piano they correlate at −0.28), so a mono sum would cancel part of the sound; and both channels together would not fit the 8 MB this page was given. The left is also the channel the honesty measure reads.

A, first half

Bass under the tine piano, with the reverb switched on

Settled promises a held note comes to rest: late (the last three of ten seconds held) at most 0.015, and at most half the early. With its switch pressed (Mix 15) the preset reads up to 0.0187 late on the upper hand, depending only on where the key lands. The phrase: C2 under a C major triad (C4 E4 G4) held, and C5 E5 G5 E5 over it, then let go.

Listen for whether the held chord settles under the melody or keeps a shimmer, and whether the reverb sounds part of the piano or a room round it.

B

The voices' budget, refitted to the core

The governor gives copies and notes up by a model of what each voice costs, fitted to the page's JavaScript. Timed in the core as WebAssembly (a WASI build of budget_cpp in node, blocks of 128, budget.py's twenty cases, two runs), four of the model's terms are off by more than the runs' own scatter, as web/docs/budget-on-the-core.md found:

TermMeasured, of the medianThe model nowFitted
Drawbars, one copy1.60, 1.705.510.7
Drawbars, each of seven copies1.43, 1.545.38.2
Pink noise0.62, 0.6230.9
The voice filter0.77, 0.5841.7

Each fitted term moves its case to the median (a unit is 0.35 ms of work a second in WebAssembly here); the filter's is the filtered saw less the saw. The other sixteen cases read 0.72 to 1.4 in both runs and are left as they are. The fit was applied in a scratch copy of core/include only. The governor is a model, not a clock, so what it gives up is the same every run whatever the machine is doing.

Drawbars, seven copies, two hands, a full chord

Split at C4: C3 E3 G3 B3 under E4 G4 B4 D5, held, with E5 G5 B5 D6 over them. At the instrument's own budget of 350 the governor acts in both.

Listen to the chorus of the copies: as it is the chord keeps most of its width; refitted it is thinner and closer to a plain organ, and thins again under each melody note.

The governor as it is
Costs fitted to the core
  • As it is: seven copies a hand while the chord alone sounds; under the melody the lower hand 5 to 7, the upper 6 to 7 copies. Notes let go: none.
  • Refitted: the lower hand 3 to 4 copies and the upper 4 to 5 from the moment the chord lands, and one fewer in the lower hand under each melody note. Notes let go: none.

Pink noise through the filter, two hands

Split at C4: C2 G2 C3 E3 G3 under D4 G4 C5, held, with D5 E5 G5 C6 over them. Pink noise and the filter never reach 350 units in either model (eight notes cost 76 as it is and 41 refitted), so both builds were given a budget of 45 to make the governor act; what differs is how many notes it lets go.

Listen to the chord's body: as it is, half the chord's noise bands go as it lands and the hand under the melody is thin; refitted, only one inner note goes, and only when the melody enters.

The governor as it is (budget 45)
Costs fitted to the core (budget 45)
  • As it is: held notes let go, inner voices first: lower G2, lower C3, lower E3, upper G4, upper C5. The chord lands as four notes (C2, G3, D4, C5), C5 goes when the melody enters, and each melody note sounds over C2, G3 and D4.
  • Refitted: held notes let go: lower G2, when the melody enters; the other seven of the chord sound under it.

Reading: the refit is audible. It is safer for the drawbars (they are the side that glitches) at the cost of their width under any chord of eight, and kinder to pink noise and the filter, which today give notes up early. At the real budget only the drawbars change; the noise case needs a budget far below 350 to differ at all.

How it was made: the honesty readings are core/tests/tools/honesty_cpp.cpp at 48 kHz (the rate that reproduces the 0.009 to 0.019 the after-stage-5 note gives), the key at sixteen places from 0.900 s to 1.134 s after the load, a sixty-fourth of a second apart, with honestytest.py's summary and rules. The scripts are in make/ beside this page.
