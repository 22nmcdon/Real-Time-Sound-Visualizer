The Plane's starting setting

17 Nov. Until now the Plane's switch, with nothing kept, only opened the module, so pressing it did nothing you could hear. The delay starts at a Mix of 35, the chorus and phaser at 30, the reverb at 15; the Plane has no Mix, so its start is one of its shapings. Three were rendered offline by the core and measured; the switch now gives Twist 15. Overrule it by ear if you hear otherwise.

The three

Chosen

Twist 15 (54° at full scale)

The loud parts swirl and the quiet middle stays still: a stereo-linked bend, heard at once, and the knob it moves is on the module's own line, so you see what the switch did.

Every Settled probe stayed Settled on all five key landings, and every Breathing one Breathing. Heard and seen the most of the three that kept Settled.

Radial clip at 0.30

A soft clipper that keeps the stereo image, the figure rounded towards a circle. Its knobs are under the arrow, so the line would show nothing moved.

Kept every Settled probe, but barely touches the quiet sine presets (Tine piano: a spectrum change of 0.006, against the twist's 0.033): on the presets that most need the switch to be heard, it is the faintest.

Turn 25°

The figure and the pair turned whole, exact and linear: heard only as balance and image, never as a harmonic.

Took the Tine piano out of Settled on one landing of five (late 0.016 against the ceiling's 0.015), through the loop reading a turned picture. Out.
