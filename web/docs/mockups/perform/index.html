Perform

Everything named, the clock in a sentence, and what the arpeggiator will play drawn before you hold a chord.

Live: choose a pattern, a step and the octaves, and the drawing shows what a held C major chord will play over one bar. Start the clock and it counts at the tempo. Glide and Tempo are knobs (drag, wheel or arrows); the morph stays a crossfader.

Window

What changed

The arpeggiator
The three menus are named Pattern, Step and Octaves, each name over its menu, as Play’s Expression row has it. A drawing over them shows one bar of what a held C major chord plays: one row per note, one block per step. With the pattern off it draws the chord held.
The clock
One sentence: Internal · bar 5, beat 4 · Stopped. Beside it, four beat dots and Start or Stop, whichever it will do. Each part keeps a box of one width, so the button stays put while the bar count grows.
Knobs
Glide and Tempo are knobs, with Tap beside Tempo. Glide dims while Quantise is off, since it acts only then.
The morph stays a slider
CLAUDE.md lists the setup morph’s crossfader among the controls that stay sliders: it slides between two ends, and a knob would hide which end it is nearer. Its ends are named: A and B, each with Store under it, and the position is read in per cent over the fader.
Labels
Every menu, switch and knob has a name you can see, three words or fewer. Quantise the generator’s pitch is Quantise pitch; the rest of it is the info line’s.
Kept
Every id, with its type, range and options (the check compares them with testing). The clock’s three spans keep theirs (clockFrom, transportAt, and transportPlay the button), and one span is new: transportState.
Size
The panel opens where it does now and is 640 px wide. It is shorter than testing’s: 319 px tall on the full face (from 332) and 278 compact (from 324), because the labels sit over the menus where the old labels took rows of their own. It ends above the Picture strip at every window.
