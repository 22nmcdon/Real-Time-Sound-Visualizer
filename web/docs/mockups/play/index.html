Play, three ways

The Keyboard section asks three questions in one heap: how notes sound, how they are shared between two sounds, and how they are drawn. Here are three ways to ask them apart.

Each layout is drawn at the Play tab's real size, its cards and its three columns, with the Generator, Amp envelope and Voice filter where they are today. Every menu and switch works, the editors open and close, and the hands diagram plays: use A–K and W E T Y U on your keyboard, or the keys under the mockup. The dealing follows the instrument's rules: rank at the start, lowest or highest N, the Chord window gathering notes by pitch, and a note never changing hand once its gather closes. The face is in the instrument's one light; only this page follows yours.
