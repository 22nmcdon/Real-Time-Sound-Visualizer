Scope & measure

The oscilloscope’s own tools, read one subject at a time: Trigger, Time, Vertical, Measure.

Live: turn the knobs (drag, wheel or arrows; double-click resets), press the switches, choose a subtab. Point at anything and the info line along the bottom says what it does - the page’s own words. Run, the mode and Autoset stay in the panel’s head on every subtab.

Window

What changed

Four subtabs
Trigger, Time, Vertical, Measure, each its name and a summary, in the figure-area bar Play and Ink use. One view at a time, in one box, so nothing moves when one is chosen. The trace filter is Vertical’s, beside the lanes (a scope’s bandwidth limit lives on its channel menu); the lag and from the note are Time’s.
Always in view
Run, Auto · Normal · Single and Autoset are the head, with the name and the close.
Knobs
All fourteen sliders are knobs, the page’s range inputs under them as on the Bench. No slider is left in the panel.
Short labels
Every label is three words or fewer and one line. Take the lag and the tuner from the note played and its orphan … and the timebase are two switches, Pitch and Timebase, under From the keys; the long sentence is each one’s info line. The checkboxes are drawn as switches; each is still its checkbox, id and all.
Drawings
Trigger draws the trace with the level dashed, the position marked over it, the crossing and the holdoff; past the trace’s peak it goes grey, as free run does. Time draws the window and the slice Zoom keeps; Vertical the two lanes at their scale and offset, and the trace filter’s response; Measure the readouts and the cursors as they will appear.
Kept
Every id the panel had, with its type, range, step and options (the check compares them with testing), so the plugin and the tests read what they read.
Height
The panel is one height at each window, ending above the Picture strip so the info line it relies on stays in view: 520 px, 469 at 1200 × 680 and 1100 × 680. Testing’s wall was 564 and, at 960 × 600, ran 5 px over the strip.
