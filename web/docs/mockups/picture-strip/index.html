The Picture strip

The same height along the bottom, with room for the info line, a plain switch, one way to more, and meters you can read.

Live: point at any knob in the strip and the info line says what it does, whole. The menu below puts any control’s line in it, among them the longest the page has. Turn the Glow, Motion and Memory reads with the last switch to see that nothing moves.

The line for Reads

What changed

Same height
90 px, and 80 compact, as on testing. Nothing else in the window moves.
The whole window’s width
The strip used to stop at the page’s 1180 px measure (.wrap). Now it runs edge to edge, with the brand’s 24 px gutters, as the modulation row above it already does. At 1660 × 1050 that gives the info line 480 px more, and 220 px more at 1400 × 900.
The info line, whole
The name and the value go on one line, with More at its end. Under them is the control’s whole line, up to four lines of it, at 12.5 px, or 11.5 compact, in Jost 400. Every one of the page’s lines fits whole at all six windows. The check sets each one in turn: 391 lines, the longest 150 characters. On testing the sentence shared one line with the name and was cut after about 40 characters.
The switch, plainly
It reads Width is · Only heard · Drawn too, with the label over the switch. Testing had the label under the switch, as a caption that read like a third option (Width on). The ids are widthHeard and widthDrawn, as they were.
One way to more
More (infoMore) opens the card, as it does now. Right-click for more is gone from the strip. Right-click still opens every control’s menu, and the More button’s title and the card both say so.
The three reads
Glow, Motion and Memory are each a row: the name in 11 px --text, a 64 px bar (52 compact) filled in charcoal, and the reading in per cent, in figures of one width. Testing drew them as three 7 px slivers in --line, with their names in 10 px under the slivers.
Not changed
The seven knobs, their order and their host dots, Advanced, and the output meter beside the line.
