The owner’s notes, and what answers each

Clipped
Key · Notes · Layer sat on the strip’s top edge, half under it. It is now the first thing in a row of its own over the strip, the band’s right column’s first row, which is there (unseen) with one hand too, so choosing Two hands moves nothing. Nothing is laid over the keys in any state. The checks hold the switch clear of the strip, and the row’s contents inside the row with nothing cut, at every window.
By notes
The keys stay. Each held note is in the ink of the hand it was dealt to, numbered from the counted end (1 the lowest, or the highest), and a dashed rule falls between the last counted note and the first not. How many is six numbered places in the split row, beside Lowest · Highest, so the count can be chosen with nothing held. The octave buttons stay in every way of sharing, because the keys are there to scroll.
low · high
A two-way switch in the split row, Lowest · Highest, one width each, not a squeezed pill at the strip’s left. It cost the strip 40 px of keys on testing; the keys are the right column’s whole width now, in every state.
The crowded line
The line under the strip is one sentence, which fits on one line at every window, and the strip’s octave (‹ C3 – B5 ›) at its right end. Learn from a key is in the split row by Key, after Split at C4; the Chord window with its value is in the split row by Notes, at its right end, and says it is Settings’.
Room
The band is 150 px (from 122) on the full face and 130 compact (from 117), and the keys take what is left: 84 px tall (from 44) and 75 compact (from 36), the right column’s whole width. Expression alone is one row of four under it, 47 px (from 67) and 40 compact (from 60), its title in the band’s left column. So the figure area is within 8 px of the last mockup’s on the full face and 7 px larger compact, and keeps its floors: the tightest drawing is the filter’s envelope stacked at 1660 × 1050, 120 px exactly, and Wave’s cycle at 960 × 600, 130.
Wave
The Generator’s Shape, Glide, Interval and Level are Wave’s header, each name beside its control in one line, with the link pill and the dots (Tuning) at its end, so Wave’s header is no taller than the others’ and the cycle keeps its size. Then the cycle, then the shape’s own band. Its summary is still the shape and its main setting.

Nothing moves

The checks compare every box on Play to half a pixel (the band, its heading, split row and each thing in it, the keys, the line, the octave; the Expression row and each field; the figure area, its bar, its subtabs and the view’s header, tools, drawings and knobs) across: one hand and two in Dyad, Mono and Poly; Key, Notes and Layer; each count from 1 to 6 from either end; a chord held and none; Learn waiting; every Wave shape; and the three subtabs; at all six windows. The nulls, each seen to fail: the band 6 px taller with two hands, as testing had it; the octave buttons taken out by notes, as testing does for low · high; Key · Notes · Layer on the strip’s top edge, as testing has it; and a Wave band sized to its shape.

What I would change my mind on

  • The keys’ height. I gave the keys every pixel Expression gave back and 8 more on the full face. At 1660 × 1050 that puts the stacked filter at its 120 px floor exactly; 10 px less band would buy it back and still leave the keys 74 tall.
  • Six places for how many, rather than a stepper. Six is what the instrument deals, and a row of numbers says that, but it is the widest thing in the split row: at 1100 × 860, the full face with the narrowest bench, the row has 11.5 px to spare, which is why the switches there are the small ones and the band’s left column 216 px (from 236).
  • No “to the lower hand” in the split row. It said what the line under the keys says, and it was the 115 px the row did not have. The coloured keys and the numbers say it a third time.
  • The Chord window named in the band at all. It is Settings’, and set once; if the band should only hold what is played, it can go back to Settings with a line in the Notes hint pointing there.
