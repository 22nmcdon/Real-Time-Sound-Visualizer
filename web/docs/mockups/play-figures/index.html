Round 2: the spec

The owner approved the direction (18 Nov) and asked for four things, all here: the Envelope out of Play and into its own editor over the tabs; the settings row’s values shortened so nothing is cut; nothing on Play moves when anything on it is selected; and the build’s spec written down, web/docs/play-figures.md. Everything below is measured in the brand’s own faces (Jost, Playfair Display), which the checks now load; the first round’s numbers were taken in a fallback face and differed by a pixel or two.

The gap, found

Not the hidden knob store and not the popover: both are out of the flow, and taking both out of the page leaves the gap where it was. .bench-body became a wrapping flex row on 17 Nov (flex-wrap: wrap, scope.html line 1630) so the band could be its first line and the columns its second; with align-content left at normal a wrapping flex container stretches its lines, so the spare height under the columns is shared between the two, and the band has half of it under it. At 1660 × 1050 that is 124 px (the 12 px row gap and half of 224 spare); at 1400 × 900, 49. It grows with the window’s height. The fix is .bench-body { align-content: flex-start; }, which leaves the row gap and nothing more (checks/gap_cause.py, with nulls). The popover’s drawing at 1660 × 1050 is 254 × 427, the tower; its running past the window I could not reproduce here.

What Play is

  • The Keyboard band, at the page’s one-hand height (122 px, 117 compact), whatever the mode or the hands. Two hands is the pill in the Keyboard’s heading, beside its name, Poly only (in Dyad and Mono it keeps its place unseen). I chose to reserve the room rather than lay things over the strip: Key · Notes · Layer sits centred on the strip’s top edge in a 12 px margin that is there either way, and the hint under the strip always has two lines’ room. The tallest state, Poly with two hands, then fits the one-hand height with 1.3 px to spare on the full face and 8.3 compact, so the band never grows and the figure area keeps the one-hand size.
  • The settings row 8 px under it (6 compact): Expression’s four menus and the Generator’s Shape, Glide, Interval and Level, 67 px tall (60 compact), the columns weighted to what each holds. Every value fits its menu at all six windows (below).
  • The figure area: two subtabs, Amp and Filter, each label its name and its one-line summary. Amp: Shape…, Loop, Hold a note in the header, the drawing full width, its six knobs. Filter: the type in the header, the response with its four knobs and its envelope with its six, side by side unless stacking leaves each 120 px tall (only 1660 × 1050).
  • The link pills sit at the right end of each header, beside the dots, and are one width (98 px) whatever they say, so their appearing with Two hands and their words changing move nothing else.
  • The Envelope is a modulation source, so it has no subtab: its card opens its editor over the tabs, as the LFOs’ do, the bench’s full width and height, in the chosen drawing style: the drawing the editor’s width and held at 2.4:1 (2.6:1 compact), its six knobs under it, and Where it goes taking the rest of the height, scrolling inside itself past what fits. It follows the amp envelope, dashed and grey, until edited; Follow puts it back. Given the whole height beside Where it goes, as the LFOs’ editors lay out, its drawing was a tower again (588 × 630 at 1660 × 1050), which is why it is stacked.

Nothing moves

The owner’s rule: selecting something must not shift the view. The checks compare every box on Play, 40 of them with the amp shown (the band, its strip, its hint and its mode switch; the settings row and each field and its control; the figure area, its bar and both subtabs; the view’s header, each tool, the drawing and every knob cell), to half a pixel, across: Two hands on and off in Dyad, Mono and Poly; Loop, Sustain here, No sustain and a note held; each filter type (Off says the filter is off); the filter envelope’s Loop; both link pills; Amp and Filter (outside the view); and the Envelope’s editor opening over the tabs. All hold at all six windows. The nulls: the band 6 px taller with two hands, as testing has it, and the link pill put before the tools, are each seen to move something. The check found one real shift on the way: the chosen subtab covered the bar’s rule with a 1 px negative margin, so both subtabs changed height on every switch; it is a shadow now.

The aspect, held

Every drawing is wider than tall at every window. The amp envelope runs from 2.2:1 (802 × 367 at 1660 × 1050) to 5.1:1 (810 × 159 at 1200 × 680), and is the same size with two hands. The filter’s two run from 1.6:1 (340 × 213 at 1100 × 860, the squarest) to 2.9:1 side by side, and 6.5:1 stacked at 1660 × 1050. The Envelope’s editor holds 2.4:1, 2.6:1 compact (683 × 263 at 960 × 600). The checks hold every drawing between 1.5:1 and 7:1, the amp’s and the Envelope’s at 2:1 or wider, and none under 120 px tall. At 960 × 600 the figure area is 709 × 264 and the amp drawing 689 × 145; if that is too short, Expression’s four folding behind its dots would give about 30 px.

What I would change my mind on

  • Reserving the band’s room costs nothing measurable (the band is the page’s one-hand height already), but the hint has two lines’ room on every state, so with one sound there is a blank line under the strip. Laying Key · Notes · Layer and the hint over the strip instead would buy that line back for the figures, at the cost of covering the keys’ tops.
  • The Envelope’s editor stacked rather than in the LFOs’ two columns. If the owner wants the four cards’ editors alike, the cost is a drawing nearly square at the bench’s height, or one capped at 2.4:1 with empty paper under it.
  • Side by side for the filter, except at 1660 × 1050, which costs the filter view looking different there.
