# Two corners the other scenarios step round. A note struck on an envelope
# resting wide open (reset to one, as a generator nobody plays is) has no
# attack to run and goes straight into its decay. And a time shorter than a
# sample - 0.015 ms is 0.72 of one at 48 kHz - which arrives in one step.
#
# That second one cannot tell the one-sample floor in `poleFor` from any
# lower floor, and no scenario can: a stage's pole is worked out so that it
# lands on its end in exactly `samples` steps, so at one sample it arrives on
# the first, and anything shorter overshoots and is clamped to the same
# value. The floor is there so a time of nought is not a division by it.
rate 48000
tone attackMs 50
tone decayMs 40
tone sustain 0.5
tone releaseMs 30
reset 1
at 0 gate 1 1
at 3000 gate 0
at 4600 tone attackMs 0.015
at 4600 gate 1 0.9
at 4610 gate 0
run 6000
